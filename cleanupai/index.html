Swipe to clean

Keep it or clear it.
One swipe.

Hold on to the photos that matter. Let go of the screenshots, blurry shots and videos you no longer need. A big camera roll becomes a series of quick, easy calls.

Cleanup is a swipe photo cleaner built around one gesture. Each photo fills the screen, so you can see exactly what you are deciding on. Swipe right to keep it and left to mark it for deletion. If you would rather tap, the Keep and Delete buttons do the same job.

A strip of thumbnails shows what is coming next, and an undo button takes you back a step when your thumb moves faster than your mind.

Month by month

Small sessions, big space back

Thousands of photos feel like too much to face. Take them a month at a time and watch your free space grow.

Every month shows how many files it holds and how much space can be released, so you know where a session will pay off most. A progress ring tracks how far through the month you are. Start with last month while it is still fresh, or go back to the oldest one and work forward. A few minutes at a time is enough to get back to a clean camera roll, even one that has been growing for years.

  1. Choose a month

    See how many files it holds and how much space you could free.

  2. Keep or delete

    Right to keep, left to delete. Undo if you change your mind.

  3. Check, then confirm

    Check what you marked before anything is removed.

Compress videos

Keep the video.
Lose the weight.

Videos take up more room than anything else on your phone. Shrink the big ones, pick the quality you want, and see the new size before you start.

Choose low, medium or high compression and Cleanup shows the size before and after. When you compress videos this way, you free up storage without giving up the moments themselves. It is the quickest win when your iPhone storage is nearly full and you do not want to delete anything.

Duplicates & similar photos

Ten takes. Keep the best one.

Identical and look-alike photos are grouped together so you can choose the ones worth keeping.

Cleanup works as a duplicate photo remover for exact copies and as a finder for burst shots, repeated screenshots and near-identical takes. You compare each group, pick the keepers, and only the rest are removed.

Merge contacts

One person. One contact.

Find repeated contacts, check the merged version, and tidy your address book in one go.

Entries for the same person are listed together, with the merged result shown first. Tick the ones to combine, then merge them all at once.

Secret space

Private photos stay private.

Move personal photos and videos into a passcode-locked space inside the app.

Set a passcode, choose what to hide, and those photos and videos stay behind the lock until you enter it.

Your call

You approve every deletion

Swiping left only marks a photo. You review the full selection first, and you can change your mind before anything is removed. Miracle Apps does not sell your personal photos or files.

The same rule applies across the app. Whether you are clearing duplicates, shrinking videos or merging contacts, you see what will change and you confirm it. A photo cleaner should save you time, not make choices for you.

How it works

Step 1

Open the app

Install the Cleanup app from the App Store or Google Play. It asks for access to photos, videos, contacts or files only when you use a feature that needs them.

Step 2

Pick a tool

Start with swipe cleaning, or go straight to duplicates, similar photos, large videos, contacts or your secret space. Each tool does one job, so you always know what you are about to change.

Step 3

Review and confirm

Look over what you have marked. Nothing is removed, compressed or merged until you say so. Deleted photos follow your device's own rules, including Recently Deleted where supported.

Good to know before you start

Will the app delete photos automatically?

No. You review and confirm what should be removed.

Can I compare similar photos first?

Yes. The review flow is designed to help you choose the version you want to keep.

Where do deleted photos go?

Deletion follows the photo-library behavior provided by your device, including Recently Deleted where supported.

Does it work for large videos?

Yes. The app surfaces storage-heavy videos so you can review or compress them.

How do I delete duplicate photos on iPhone?

Open Cleanup and choose Find Duplicates. Identical photos are grouped together, you choose which ones to keep, and the rest are removed once you confirm.

How do I free up storage without deleting photos?

Compress your largest videos. You pick the quality, see the size before and after, and keep the video.

Can I undo a swipe?

Yes. Use the undo button to go back, and review everything you marked before anything is removed.

Is Cleanup free?

Cleanup is free to download. Some features may require Pro access or an in-app purchase, and free users may see ads depending on the app version. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. Cleanup is available on Google Play for Android and on the App Store for iPhone.

What access does Cleanup need?

Cleanup may request access to photos, videos, contacts or files only when you use a feature that needs them. That access is used to find clutter, compress media, organize items and show cleanup suggestions.
