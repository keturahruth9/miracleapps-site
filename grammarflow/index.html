FAQs

Good questions.

Will it type in my messages, notes, email and AI tools?

Yes. GrammarFlow types into the field that had focus when you pressed the key, so it works in every app with no setup. If a field can’t be typed into, the text goes to your clipboard and the bar offers Copy again.

How is it different from built-in dictation?

It cleans as it goes: fillers and repeats are dropped and punctuation is added, so the sentence reads like you wrote it. Afterwards, the same words can be fixed, re-toned or translated from the selection popup.

What does it need on a Mac?

Microphone and Accessibility permission, both asked for during setup. The Dictation page offers to set the fn key to “Do nothing”, so holding it stops opening the emoji picker.

And on Windows?

Microphone permission. Hold Ctrl and Win together to talk. There is no separate keyboard permission on Windows.

Is my voice data stored?

No. Audio stays in memory, is sent only for transcription, and is never stored. Your history is encrypted on your computer, per account, and you can turn it off.

Is GrammarFlow free?

You get 10 credits once per account. Previewing a result is free; copying or inserting a new one takes a credit, and reusing it doesn’t. Premium removes the count.

Does it share an account with the iPhone app?

Yes. Sign in with the same account, and credits and Premium are one wallet across iPhone and desktop.

How do I stop or cancel quickly?

Press Esc or the X on the bar to cancel. Tap the key twice within half a second for hands-free, click the bar or press the key once to finish, and a hold under half a second sends nothing.
