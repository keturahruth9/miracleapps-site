Questions people ask

Which TVs does it work with?

LG webOS, Samsung, Android TV and Google TV, Roku, Fire TV, Sony Bravia, Philips, Panasonic, Vizio, Mi TV and Sky Q. TCL and Hisense TVs work when they run Google TV, Roku or Fire TV. Your TV needs to be on Wi-Fi: older TVs with an infrared-only remote are not supported.

Do I need the original remote to set it up?

Usually not. Your TV has to be switched on and already connected to Wi-Fi. Some TVs show a prompt or a code the first time, which you can accept with the buttons on the TV itself.

Why can't the app find my TV?

Check that the phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network and that the TV is on. Guest networks often block devices from seeing each other. You can also add the TV by its IP address, which you will find in the TV's network settings.

Can it turn my TV on?

It can turn most TVs off. Turning one on depends on the TV: many switch off their Wi-Fi in standby, so they cannot hear the app until they are on.

Does voice search work in every app?

Your words are sent to the text field that is selected on the TV. Some TV apps only accept typing from their own remote. On LG TVs, searching YouTube by voice and keyboard is supported.

Does it work on a tablet?

Yes. The remote grows to fill an iPad or Android tablet.

Is it free?

The app is free to download and has no ads.
