All TV Remotes: UniversalPrivacy Policy

Overview

All TV Remotes: Universal turns your phone or tablet into a remote control for smart TVs on your home Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends to your TV, and how microphone and local-network access are used.

Data the app does not collect

Miracle Apps does not collect personal data from All TV Remotes: Universal. The app has no account, no analytics and no advertising. We do not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

The TVs you connect to (name, network address and the pairing key your TV issues) and your settings, such as language and colour theme, are saved on your device so the app can reconnect. This information is not sent to Miracle Apps.

What the app sends to your TV

Remote-control commands, text you type and app-launch requests go directly from your device to your TV over your local Wi-Fi network. They are not routed through Miracle Apps servers.

Local network access

The app asks for local-network access to find TVs on your Wi-Fi and to talk to the TV you choose. It does not scan for or contact other kinds of devices.

Microphone and voice typing

If you use voice typing, the app asks for microphone and speech-recognition access when you tap the microphone. Your speech is converted to text by the speech service on your device, provided by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, and only the resulting text is sent to your TV. The microphone is used only after you tap the microphone button, until you stop.

Ratings and store links

The app may ask you to rate it using the rating prompt provided by the App Store or Google Play. Any rating or review you submit is handled by Apple or Google.

Your choices and deletion

You can forget a TV at any time in the app, which deletes its saved pairing key. You can turn off microphone, speech-recognition and local-network access in your device settings. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children’s privacy

All TV Remotes: Universal is not directed to children under 13, and Miracle Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, platform requirements, or applicable services change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
